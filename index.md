# PlanStead Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

PlanStead ("PlanStead", "we", "our", or "the App") helps you organize properties, assets, and maintenance activities. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PlanStead.

## 1. Information You Add to PlanStead

You may enter information such as property names and types, asset details, maintenance tasks and schedules, completion status, and maintenance history.

This information is used to provide the App's features. PlanStead does not require you to enter a property address to use its basic property-management features.

## 2. Local Storage and iCloud Sync

PlanStead stores its database models on your device. PlanStead also uses Apple's iCloud/CloudKit to synchronize supported database data across your devices when iCloud is available and enabled.

Your data is associated with your Apple/iCloud account and is handled by Apple under its terms and privacy practices. Sync availability and timing depend on your iCloud account, device settings, network connection, and Apple's services.

When you delete property or other database information within PlanStead, the App's deletion behavior applies to that information and, where supported by CloudKit synchronization, the deletion is synchronized to your other devices. Deleting PlanStead from your device does not necessarily delete data already stored in iCloud. You can manage your iCloud account and data through Apple's settings and services.

iCloud synchronization is not a separate backup guarantee. Keep independent copies of information that is important to you.

Learn more in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

PlanStead uses **Firebase Analytics** for general understanding of how the App is used and **Firebase Crashlytics** to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems.

We do not intentionally use these tools to collect names, property addresses, or other information that you enter into your PlanStead database. Depending on the Firebase configuration and your device settings, Firebase services may process app interaction events, app and device technical information, diagnostic data, and identifiers used for analytics or crash reporting.

We use this information to understand general app usage, improve reliability, and fix errors. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Provide property, asset, maintenance-task, schedule, and history features.
- Synchronize supported database information through iCloud.
- Understand general usage and improve the App.
- Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
- Provide and maintain the App's functionality.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by the following service providers as needed to operate the App:
- **Apple iCloud/CloudKit**, for synchronization of supported database data.
- **Firebase Analytics**, for general app usage analytics.
- **Firebase Crashlytics**, for crash diagnostics.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the App, its users, or our legal rights.

## 6. Purchases and Subscriptions

If PlanStead offers in-app purchases or subscriptions, transactions are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment details; PlanStead does not receive your full payment-card information. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. Purchases are subject to Apple's applicable terms and policies.

## 7. Notifications

PlanStead may send maintenance reminders if you enable notifications. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

PlanStead database information remains on your device and, for supported data, may also be stored in iCloud for synchronization. You can delete information through the App's available deletion controls. Deletions are synchronized to other devices where supported by CloudKit.

Deleting the App does not necessarily remove data already stored in iCloud. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics information may be retained by Google according to the relevant service configuration and retention policies.

## 9. Security

PlanStead relies on security features provided by iOS and the services it uses, including Apple and Google services, to help protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and Apple Account.

## 10. Children's Privacy

PlanStead is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been handled inappropriately, contact us using the details below.

## 11. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can manage your PlanStead database information through the App's available controls and manage iCloud through your Apple Account settings.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email below.

## 12. International Processing

Apple and Google may process information in countries other than your country of residence, in accordance with their applicable terms and safeguards.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. We will update the "Last updated" date when we publish a revised version.
